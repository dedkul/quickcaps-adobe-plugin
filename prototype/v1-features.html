Always on, no switch: your review is saved as you edit, so closing the panel or Premiere never loses work (the "Continue editing" card). Long videos send 3 audio parts to Sarvam at a time; Sarvam bills per second of audio, so the cost is identical, it's just about 3× faster.

Cost line: based on the selected audio length at ₹30/hour; it doubles and turns amber when the English track is on. Picking "English" as the caption script hides the English-track switch (it would be the same thing twice).
