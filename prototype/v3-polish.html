The preview you already have becomes the style picker. Plain stays a normal caption track; styled ones arrive as motion graphics clips on a video track.

What changed and why

  • Everything lines up. Header, content, and the Generate bar now share one 16 px edge on both sides. The middle used to be pushed over by the space reserved for the scrollbar; the panel now measures the scrollbar and tucks it into the right margin. Turn on the guides to check.
  • Less scrolling. Caption source becomes a three-way switch like the others (it was a tall list). Extras fold into one row that says what's on, so the main screen fits without scrolling on a normal docked panel. It opens in place, no popup.
  • Spoken language loses the repeated name on the right and the line under it. The menu already says it.
  • The API key behaves like a connection, not a form. Pasting checks and saves the key at once (Enter does the same, and Save key is there for anyone who types it). You always see the result: Checking, then Connected with the last four characters, or a plain reason it failed. The full key is never shown again; Change and Remove sit next to it. This month's usage moved into the same block, since it is that account's spending.
  • One way to reach you. "Follow" and "Request a feature" both went to the same page, so they're now one button with your handle, under "Made by Ashish" and a line saying what to send. The version moves to the bottom as small print, where people look for it.
  • Settings in groups (Sarvam account, Transcription, about) divided by thin lines instead of floating labels.
  • Notices drop the coloured stripe down their left edge for a soft tint and an icon. The stripe is the most common tell of AI-made interfaces.
  • "No extra cost" badges become "Free", and "2× Sarvam usage" becomes "2× cost", so badges fit beside the name on narrow panels.

Caption styles later (frame 3)

  • The preview frame becomes the style picker, so nothing else on the screen moves when styles arrive. The code for this release will already draw the preview from a "style" description, so adding styles later is adding entries, not rebuilding the screen.
  • Plain stays a normal Premiere caption track. A styled caption becomes a motion graphics template (.mogrt) clip per caption on a video track, with the text filled in by QuickCaps.
